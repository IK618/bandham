## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid (Stateful Reasoning Engine + Structured Extraction + Content Generation + Real-Time Safety Guardrails)

**Description:**
An AI-powered matrimonial matchmaking intelligence and trust biometrics system for Bandhan Matrimonial. It evaluates multi-dimensional candidate compatibility against structured partner preferences, generates culturally respectful biodata narrative enhancements, verifies webcam selfies against government ID documents using facial biometric liveness detection, and acts as an online safety guardrail monitoring real-time chat interactions for harassment, unsolicited contact leaks, and policy violations.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. **Dealbreaker Hallucination / Misalignment**: Falsely stating compatibility on hard cultural dealbreakers (e.g., claiming a strict Jain vegetarian is compatible with a non-vegetarian, or misrepresenting marital status / Manglik dosha).
2. **Safety Barrier & Contact Leakage Bypass**: Permitting candidates to leak unverified phone numbers, external messenger links, or vulgar solicitations in chat prior to mutual consent and official credit reveal.
3. **Biometric Liveness & Identity Fraud**: Accepting 2D screen replays, printed portraits, or deepfakes during webcam verification, or mistakenly verifying mismatched photos against government credentials.
4. **Culturally Inappropriate Narrative Tone**: Generating flippant, casual dating jargon (e.g., "hookup vibes", "casual fling") that offends prospective candidates and family elders in serious marriage arrangements.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in Indian matrimonial and cultural domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** Indian Matrimonial & Matchmaking Services (Arranged, Semi-Arranged, and Self-Choice Courtship)

**User Population:** Prospective brides, grooms, parents, siblings, and family guardians across India and the global NRI diaspora.

**Stakes Level:** High (Marriage involves permanent legal, financial, familial, and emotional commitments; errors trigger social stigma, family friction, or safety liabilities).

**Output Consequence:** Direct impact on formal family introductions, personal meeting arrangements, verified contact disclosures, and legal matrimony alliances.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

1. **Dimension: Dealbreaker Rigidity (Diet, Religion, Marital Status)**
   - **Good**: 100% adherence to candidate's specified non-negotiable boundaries. If a user marks "Strict Vegetarian Only", non-vegetarian candidates are immediately disqualified from high-tier recommendations.
   - **Bad**: Recommending candidates with incompatible dietary or religious lifestyles and explaining it away as "opposites attract".
   - **Stakes**: Critical.
   - **Source**: Indian Matrimonial Practitioner Standard; dietary and community preferences are sacred criteria in 85%+ of Indian households.

2. **Dimension: Cultural Decorum & Dignity**
   - **Good**: Narratives highlight educational accomplishments, career goals, personal values, family background, and mutual life vision in dignified, warm prose.
   - **Bad**: Employing dating app colloquialisms ("looking for fun", "casual dates", "swipe right"), superficial physical judgments, or overly aggressive self-promotion.
   - **Stakes**: High.
   - **Source**: Relationship Managers & Senior Matchmakers at premier matrimonial bureaus.

3. **Dimension: Anti-Dowry & Harassment Protection**
   - **Good**: Immediate, zero-tolerance interception of any messages requesting dowry, cash gifts, luxury goods, financial loans, or offensive physical advances.
   - **Bad**: Permitting implicit dowry requests ("we expect our daughter-in-law's family to handle all wedding expenses and car") to pass through unflagged.
   - **Stakes**: Critical.
   - **Source**: Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 & Indian Penal Code (Sections 498A, 354A).

4. **Dimension: Genuine Biometric Identity Verification**
   - **Good**: Facial liveness confirms real human presence via randomized motion prompts (eye blink, head tilt), followed by high-confidence facial landmark embedding alignment (cosine similarity $\ge 0.70$) against verified ID.
   - **Bad**: Authenticating a static printed photo held in front of the webcam or a photo taken of a digital tablet display.
   - **Stakes**: Critical.
   - **Source**: IT Act 2000 (Intermediary Guidelines) & Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

- **Manglik Status Conflation**: Confusing "Partial Manglik" (Angshik) with "Full Manglik", causing erroneous astrological friction between families who consult horoscopes.
- **Family Structure Inversion**: Mischaracterizing a candidate from a traditional Joint Family as seeking a Nuclear Family setup or vice-versa.
- **Pre-Consent Contact Phishing**: Subtle evasion of contact filters using obfuscated phonetic numbers ("nine eight four five zero...", "call me on my insta id @...").

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

- **DPDP Act 2023 (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, India)**: Strict consent mandates, mandatory purpose limitation for biometrics, right to erasure, and biometric template encryption.
- **Information Technology Rules, 2021**: Intermediary due diligence for user safety, prompt takedown of offensive content (< 24 hours), and secure logging.
- **Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961**: Criminal prohibition against demanding, giving, or abetting dowry.

### Domain Expert Roles for Evaluation

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **Senior Matrimonial Consultant / Relationship Manager** | Calibrates biodata narrative prompts, audits explainable compatibility justifications, and establishes cultural appropriateness rubrics. |
| **Trust & Safety Compliance Officer** | Audits online guardrail filters against dowry demands, abusive language, and unauthorized contact solicitation. |
| **Lead Computer Vision / Biometrics Engineer** | Calibrates facial landmark recognition thresholds and anti-spoofing liveness datasets. |

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** LangGraph

**Version:** `langgraph>=0.2.30` (with `langchain-core>=0.3.15`, `pydantic>=2.10.0`)

**Rationale:**
1. **Python-Native Architecture**: Integrates directly with Bandhan's existing FastAPI backend (`backend/app`), SQLAlchemy 2.0 async engine, PostgreSQL (`bandhan_postgres`), and Redis cache.
2. **Stateful Graph Orchestration**: Matrimonial evaluation requires deterministic, multi-step validation DAGs:
   $$\text{Candidate Data} \longrightarrow \text{Rule-based Filtering} \longrightarrow \text{Compatibility Scorer} \longrightarrow \text{Narrative Reasoner} \longrightarrow \text{Safety Guardrail} \longrightarrow \text{Verified Response}$$
3. **Database Checkpointing (`AsyncPostgresSaver`)**: LangGraph checkpoints state transitions at every node directly into PostgreSQL, providing a tamper-evident audit trail for safety moderation and admin disputes.
4. **First-Class Pydantic v2 Structured Outputs**: Guarantees schema adherence for explainable match breakdowns and moderation flags.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| **CrewAI** | Lacks fine-grained node-level checkpointing in relational databases; higher abstraction overhead for deterministic rule-based + LLM pipelines. |
| **LlamaIndex** | Excellent for document RAG, but secondary for complex multi-step decision graphs and real-time chat guardrails. |
| **OpenAI Agents SDK** | Hard vendor lock-in to OpenAI; violates platform requirement for model-agnostic operation (Gemini / Anthropic / local models). |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** None. LangGraph is open-source (MIT License) and operates over any standard LLM provider via `langchain-core` chat abstractions.

### Core Imports

from pydantic import BaseModel, Field
from langgraph.graph import StateGraph, START, END
from langgraph.checkpoint.postgres.aio import AsyncPostgresSaver

### Entry Point Pattern (Stateful Compatibility & Moderation Graph)
```python
class MatrimonialGraphState(TypedDict):
    current_user_id: str
    target_profile_id: str
    preferences: Dict
    candidate_profile: Dict
    dealbreaker_passed: bool
    numerical_score: float
    compatibility_reasoning: Optional[Dict]
    safety_violations: List[str]
    is_safe: bool

async def evaluate_dealbreakers(state: MatrimonialGraphState) -> Dict:
    prefs = state["preferences"]
    cand = state["candidate_profile"]
    # Strict deterministic rule checks
    if prefs.get("acceptable_diets") and cand.get("diet") not in prefs["acceptable_diets"]:
        return {"dealbreaker_passed": False, "numerical_score": 0.0}
    return {"dealbreaker_passed": True}

async def generate_compatibility_reasoning(state: MatrimonialGraphState) -> Dict:
    if not state.get("dealbreaker_passed"):
        return {"compatibility_reasoning": {"summary": "Profile does not meet essential dietary/lifestyle criteria."}}
    
    llm = ChatGoogleGenerativeAI(model="gemini-2.0-flash", temperature=0.2)
    structured_llm = llm.with_structured_output(CompatibilityAnalysis)
    
    prompt = ChatPromptTemplate.from_messages([
        ("system", "You are an expert Indian matrimonial counselor. Analyze partner compatibility with cultural dignity."),
        ("human", "Preferences: {prefs}\nCandidate: {cand}")
    ])
    result = await (prompt | structured_llm).ainvoke({
        "prefs": state["preferences"],
        "cand": state["candidate_profile"]
    })
    return {"compatibility_reasoning": result.model_dump(), "numerical_score": result.score}

def route_after_dealbreakers(state: MatrimonialGraphState) -> str:
    return "generate_reasoning" if state.get("dealbreaker_passed") else "finalize"

builder = StateGraph(MatrimonialGraphState)
builder.add_node("check_dealbreakers", evaluate_dealbreakers)
builder.add_node("generate_reasoning", generate_compatibility_reasoning)
builder.add_edge(START, "check_dealbreakers")
builder.add_conditional_edges("check_dealbreakers", route_after_dealbreakers, {
    "generate_reasoning": "generate_reasoning",
    "finalize": END
})
builder.add_edge("generate_reasoning", END)

matrimonial_ai_pipeline = builder.compile()
```

### Key Abstractions

| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `StateGraph` | Core graph compiler managing execution states, nodes, and edges | Orchestrating multi-step matchmaking, biodata enhancement, and chat guardrails |
| `TypedDict State` | Immutable schema defining data flowing between nodes | Holding user preferences, candidate profiles, and analysis payloads |
| `Conditional Edge` | Branching decision function based on state attributes | Routing directly to termination when hard dealbreakers or safety violations are detected |
| `AsyncPostgresSaver` | Async PostgreSQL checkpoint persistence engine | Storing persistent execution state in `bandhan_postgres` for safety audits |

### Common Pitfalls
1. **Mutating State In-Place**: LangGraph state nodes must return a dictionary delta of updated keys, never mutate `state` directly.
2. **Blocking the Async Event Loop**: Running heavy computer-vision biometrics (e.g., DeepFace) directly inside an async node freezes FastAPI. Always dispatch synchronous processing via `asyncio.to_thread(face_analyzer.verify, ...)`.
3. **Unbounded Generation Budget**: Generating profile narratives without explicit `max_tokens` (target $\le 300$ tokens) risks runaway costs and verbose, untidy text.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
- Primary LLM: `gemini-2.0-flash` (or `gemini-1.5-flash`) via Google Cloud API.
- Fallback LLM: `gpt-4o-mini` (OpenAI) or `claude-3-5-haiku-20241022` (Anthropic).
- Temperature: `0.1` for safety guardrails and compatibility analysis; `0.4` for biodata narrative generation.
- Max Tokens: `300` for narrative summaries; `200` for guardrail classification.

**Core Pattern:**
Stateful LangGraph DAG with early termination on dealbreakers or safety violations, backed by Pydantic v2 structured output validation.

**Biometrics Subsystem:**
Webcam capture stream $\longrightarrow$ OpenCV/DeepFace anti-spoofing liveness test (blink/texture analysis) $\longrightarrow$ ArcFace/FaceNet facial landmark embedding extraction $\longrightarrow$ Cosine similarity evaluation against government ID portrait.

**Context Window Strategy:**
Zero prompt bloat. Cleanly serializes only normalized fields (education, career, religion, diet, lifestyle, mutual interests). Never dumps raw database rows or unvetted historical chats into LLM context.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

### 4b.1 Structured Outputs with Pydantic v2

```python
from typing import List, Optional
from pydantic import BaseModel, Field

class CompatibilityDimensionScore(BaseModel):
    dimension_name: str = Field(description="e.g., Lifestyle, Education, Community, Values")
    score_out_of_100: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    alignment_reason: str = Field(description="One sentence explaining cultural or lifestyle alignment.")

class CompatibilityAnalysis(BaseModel):
    overall_compatibility_pct: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    executive_summary: str = Field(description="Warm, dignified 2-sentence matrimonial summary.")
    dimension_breakdowns: List[CompatibilityDimensionScore] = Field(min_length=3, max_length=7)
    potential_discussion_topics: List[str] = Field(description="Respectful icebreaker topics for families.")

class ChatGuardrailDecision(BaseModel):
    is_safe: bool = Field(description="True if message complies with matrimonial safety rules.")
    violation_category: Optional[str] = Field(default=None, description="DOWRY | HARASSMENT | CONTACT_LEAK | SPAM")
    sanitized_content: Optional[str] = Field(default=None, description="Redacted message if minor contact leak.")
    action_required: str = Field(description="PASS | REDACT | BLOCK | ESCALATE_ADMIN")
```

### 4b.2 Async-First Design
- Graph execution is 100% asynchronous using `.ainvoke()` across all FastAPI endpoint handlers.
- Biodata narrative enhancements support token streaming via server-sent events (SSE) for delightful frontend responsiveness.
- Database reads and writes utilize SQLAlchemy `AsyncSession` seamlessly alongside `AsyncPostgresSaver`.

### 4b.3 Prompt Engineering Discipline
- **System Prompts**: Codify sacred cultural rules, strict ethical limits, anti-dowry mandates, and dignified Indian family etiquette.
- **User Prompts**: Strictly compartmentalized input containing only sanitized profile facts.
- **Token Budget**: Fixed budgets enforced on every LLM call ($300$ tokens for bios, $200$ for analysis).

### 4b.4 Context Window Management
- Fast in-memory caching of profile vectors and candidate records.
- Redis-backed caching (`ai:match:{user_a}:{user_b}`) with a 24-hour TTL prevents wasteful duplicate model invocations.

### 4b.5 Cost and Latency Budget
- Per-call model inference target: $<\$0.00015$ using Gemini 2.0 Flash.
- Latency target: $<450\text{ms}$ for compatibility reasoning, $<150\text{ms}$ for chat safety guardrail.

### Dimensions

|-----------|-------------------------------|---------------------|----------|
| **Dealbreaker Rigidity** | **PASS**: 0% false matches on hard constraints (Diet, Marital Status).<br>**FAIL**: Suggesting incompatible diet/lifestyle as compatible. | Code-based Deterministic Check | **Critical** |
| **Cultural Tone Decorum** | **PASS**: Warm, dignified, respectful Indian matrimonial tone.<br>**FAIL**: Casual dating terms ("hookup", "casual", "hot", "flirt"). | LLM Judge (calibrated against human SME) | **High** |

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time Interceptors)

| Guardrail | Trigger Event | Intervention Action |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Dowry & Extortion Shield** | Message mentions "dowry", "cash gifts", "car demand", or wedding expenses coercion | **BLOCK** message immediately; freeze sender account; page Admin |
| **Unsolicited Phone Leak** | Regular expressions or semantic detector spots phone digits before mutual consent | **REDACT** digits; notify sender: *"Contact details unlock requires mutual consent"* |
| **Toxicity & Abuse Guard** | Vulgar insults, sexual harassment, or derogatory slurs | **BLOCK** message; increment user strike counter; flag for moderator review |
| **Facial Spoofing Rejector** | Static eye gaze, paper borders, or zero-liveness movement detected | **REJECT** selfie; request dynamic head turn & natural eye blink |

### Offline (Continuous Improvement Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Explainability Faithfulness** | Weekly batch audit of 100 random compatibility reasoning records | Retrain/refine few-shot exemplars if hallucination score exceeds 1% |
| **High-Score Match Acceptance** | Monthly cohort tracking: ratio of accepted interests where AI score $\ge 85\%$ | Re-weight preference vectors if user acceptance diverges from score |
| **Safety False-Positive Dispute** | 100% of user appeals on blocked messages reviewed by human moderators | Update exclusion lexicon and re-calibrate prompt thresholds |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Arize Phoenix (via OpenTelemetry) / Prometheus metrics endpoint.

**Key Metrics to Track:**
1. **P95 LLM Inference Latency**: Target $< 500\text{ms}$ for chat moderation, $< 1000\text{ms}$ for compatibility reasoning.
2. **Guardrail Trigger Frequency**: Baseline $0.5\% - 1.5\%$ of chat messages; alert if spike $> 5\%$.
3. **Daily Token Consumption & Cost**: Monitored per active user cohort against the $\$0.00015$/call budget.
4. **Biometric Verification Pass Rate**: Expected $88\% - 94\%$ on authentic attempts; alert if drop below $80\%$.

**Alert Thresholds:**
- P95 Latency $> 2000\text{ms}$ sustained over 5 minutes $\longrightarrow$ Page On-Call Engineer.
- Guardrail False-Positive Spike $> 3\%$ in 1 hour $\longrightarrow$ Auto-switch to conservative fallback.
