# Bandhan Matrimonial (बंधन)

## What This Is

Bandhan Matrimonial is an authentic, trust-first Indian matrimonial matchmaking platform designed to connect marriage-minded individuals and families with high cultural fidelity, strict two-way privacy, a 4-pillar Trust Shield (0–100% verification), and multi-tiered monetization.

## Core Value

Empowering families and candidates to discover life partners with absolute dignity, certified profile authenticity, and safe, consented communication.

## Business Context

- **Customer**: Marriage candidates, brides, grooms, and parents across India and the global diaspora.
- **Revenue Model**: Freemium subscription tiers (Free, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) with contact unlock credits, instant UPI QR verification, and priority search boost.
- **Success Metric**: High match-to-courtship connection rate and zero platform identity fraud.

## Current Milestones

- **Milestone 1 (v1.0 Core Platform & Trust Shield)**: Completed (Phases 1-4).
- **Milestone 2 (v2.0 Intelligent Matrimonial Platform)**: Active (Phase 5: AI Integration & Matchmaking Intelligence).

## Constraints

- Strict data privacy and DPDP/GDPR alignment.
- Bidirectional block and harassment barrier enforcement.
- Mobile accessibility with >=44px hit targets and >=16px font inputs.
